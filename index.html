Try it

A module I built, running live

This is the 13th month pay calculation from Ongkoleyt. Each month's included earnings are built up from days worked, half days, overtime and other included pay, then the January–December total is divided by twelve. Open the sample and change the inputs to see it recompute.

  • 13th month = Total included earnings (Jan–Dec) ÷ 12
  • Month total = Basic + OT + Other included
  • Basic = (Days × Daily rate) + (Half days × 0.5 × Daily rate)
  • OT = OT hours × Hourly rate × OT multiplier
